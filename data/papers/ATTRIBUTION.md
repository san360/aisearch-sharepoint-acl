[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They are redistributed here unchanged, only renamed to `<arXiv id>.pdf`, as sample content for the SharePoint ACL demo.
